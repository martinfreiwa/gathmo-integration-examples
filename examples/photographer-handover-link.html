Separating professional delivery from guest media

A photographer can recommend a guest contribution workflow without implying that crowd-sourced files replace professional coverage. The link should describe the complementary purpose: collecting candid phone photos, short clips, and messages while the photographer retains a separate delivery process.

Client-resource wording

For candid uploads from friends and family, couples can use Gathmo's browser-based wedding album. Professional gallery delivery remains separate.

Handover checklist

  • Clarify who owns and moderates the guest album.
  • Avoid promising that guest uploads meet professional quality standards.
  • Confirm whether the photographer may access or reuse guest files.
  • Send the resource early enough for the couple to add it to signage.
