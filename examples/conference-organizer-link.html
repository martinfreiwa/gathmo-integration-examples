Conference attendee media collection

Conference websites can introduce an attendee contribution link before opening the external collection. The surrounding copy should explain the destination, accepted media, and whether submissions are moderated before display.

Accessible link pattern

Share photos and short videos from the conference through the Gathmo corporate event media workflow. The contribution page opens in the browser and does not require an app install.

Implementation notes

  • Use descriptive link text instead of “click here.”
  • Keep the contribution link separate from the attendee login.
  • State whether the organizer may feature approved submissions.
  • Provide a short fallback URL next to printed QR codes.
