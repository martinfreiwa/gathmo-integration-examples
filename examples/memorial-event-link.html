Private-event integration

Linking to a private memorial media collection

Memorial contribution requests should use direct, respectful wording and avoid public-sharing assumptions. The family or organizer should decide who receives the link, whether contributions require review, and whether the resulting album remains private.

Invitation wording

If you would like to share a photo, short video, or spoken memory, use the private browser upload provided through Gathmo's event contribution workflow.

Privacy considerations

  • Distribute the album link only through the intended invitation channel.
  • State who may view contributions and whether moderation is enabled.
  • Offer a clear removal contact for contributors.
  • Avoid embedding private album identifiers in a public resource page.
