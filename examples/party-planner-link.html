Adding guest media collection to a party plan

Planner resource pages should present guest media collection as an optional operational service with a clear owner. The host needs to know who creates the album, who reviews submissions, and how long guests may continue uploading.

Service-page wording

Add an app-free QR album to the celebration with Gathmo for parties, so guests can contribute photos, videos, and messages from their mobile browser.

Activation details

  • Assign one person to test the QR code before guests arrive.
  • Use the same destination across signage, invitations, and follow-up messages.
  • Explain whether approved uploads may appear on a live display.
  • Include export and post-event access in the planner handover.
